What I collect and why

When you write to me. The contact and page teardown forms ask for your name, email and message, and the teardown form also for the page you want reviewed and its goal. I use them only to answer you and, if we agree to work together, to prepare that work. Legal basis: steps you ask for before a possible contract, and my legitimate interest in replying to messages (GDPR art. 6.1.b and 6.1.f).

Statistics, only with your consent. If you click “Accept” in the cookie banner, Google Analytics records which pages you visit, roughly where from, the device and browser, and how you arrived. It does not tell me who you are. If you decline or ignore the banner, it never loads. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time.

Technical logs. Like any server, the hosting provider records IP addresses and requests briefly to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the site running and secure (GDPR art. 6.1.f).

I don’t make automated decisions about anyone, and I don’t build profiles.

How long I keep it

Messages stay in my inbox while the conversation is useful, and are deleted at the latest two years after the last exchange — unless they become part of a project, in which case they are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Analytics data is kept by Google for a maximum of 14 months, then deleted automatically.

Server logs are kept by Vercel for a short period set by their own policy, and are not used by me.

Cookies

A cookie is a small file a site stores in your browser. This site only uses analytics cookies, and only after you accept them.

yt-cookie-consent
Remembers whether you accepted or declined, so the banner doesn’t ask again. Stored in your browser’s local storage, never sent anywhere. Necessary; kept until you clear your browser data.
_ga
Google Analytics. Tells visits from the same browser apart. Analytics; 2 years. Only after consent.
_ga_5MHWQYG4MV
Google Analytics. Keeps track of the current visit. Analytics; 2 years. Only after consent.

hCaptcha, on the contact and teardown pages, may store technical data needed to tell people from bots. It is part of the form you choose to send, not tracking.

You can change your choice whenever you want: change cookie settings. Declining after having accepted deletes the Google Analytics cookies. You can also block or delete cookies from your browser’s settings.
